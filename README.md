# Poc.mp3
A mp3 player that fit into your pocket. With expandable micro sd storage, cool analog and digital VU meter. An AUX output so everyone is happy. All on the esp32

This has 8 buttons, including:

-5 buttons for navigation: Up, Down, Left, Right, Select

-2 buttons for volume changing: Volume up/down

-1 buttons for locking/unlocking


-Features and ESP32-S3 with 8MB of PSRAM and 16MB of flash (N16R8)

-Analog VU meter and two version of digital VU meter (NCS circle and Flat)

-Able to play pretty much most popular audio file with the ability to load the MP3 embeded images

-With SDMMC 1 bit can loads image and music up to 2MB/s (under ideal conditions)

-A 3.5mm jack output with full on Stereo

-Finally a full on UI with home screen and more !
